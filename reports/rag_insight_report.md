# Customer Feedback RAG Analysis Report

## Executive Summary
This report synthesizes text mining issue detection with Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). For each top customer complaint cluster, the system retrieved relevant empirical reviews and internal operating policies to diagnose root causes and propose compliant corrective actions.

---

### Rank #1: TASTE / FOOD / KETTLE
- **Priority Score:** 77.4/100

#### 1. Root Cause Analysis
Automated analysis indicates recurring complaints centered around 'taste / food / kettle' (taste, food, kettle). The primary root cause is identified as: Shelf life expiration or warehouse storage degradation. Reviews consistently highlight unacceptable product condition upon arrival.

#### 2. Impact Assessment
Issue priority score is 77.4/100 with 39 complaints. Approximately 64.1% of reviews were 1-star ratings, creating severe friction and risk of customer attrition.

#### 3. Actionable Recommendations
- Operational Compliance: Trigger POL-FQD-002 (Inventory audit for minimum 60-day shelf life, QA barrier check).
- Customer Support: Activate instant returnless refunds per POL-RET-001 for affected accounts.
- Supplier Feedback: Flag defect logs to manufacturer QA for packaging or formulation review.
